- VIOLENCE: threats, encouragement of violence or self-harm, or graphic violence.
- ILLEGAL: promotion of illegal or dangerous acts, illegal goods, scams, or links to outside sales.
- PRIVACY: exposure of personal information such as phone numbers, home addresses, account numbers, emails, or full names of others.

## CRITERIA
VIOLATES (violation 1):
- The text contains anything defined above, even once and even if the rest is a normal review.
SAFE (violation 0):
- Honest negative opinions about the product, price, quality, color, size, fit, delivery, or seller service.
- Low ratings, disappointment, return or refund complaints written without insults.

- Informal tone, emoticons, typos, or repeated characters ("ㅋㅋㅋ", "ㅠㅠ").
